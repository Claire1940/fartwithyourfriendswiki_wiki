<Section icon={Trophy} title="Best Team Tips for River Runs">

Once the group is in the same Roblox session, success depends more on coordination than on using a special platform feature. River challenges reward players who look ahead, make controlled adjustments, and avoid conflicting instructions.

Use these tactics for Campaign, Endless River, Daily River, and Raft Race sessions:

- **Choose one primary caller.** One clear direction is easier to follow than several players shouting different turns.
- **Look ahead before turning.** Early adjustments give the raft more time to respond when the river becomes difficult.
- **Use small movements.** Repeatedly changing direction can make the group lose control of its route.
- **Keep communication short.** Simple commands such as “left,” “right,” “slow,” and “turn” are easier to process.
- **Review failed sections.** After a difficult run, identify the exact turn or obstacle that caused the problem.
- **Practice before racing.** Cooperative activities help new players understand the basic flow without immediate team pressure.

| Team habit | Benefit | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| One caller | Reduces conflicting directions | Every multiplayer run |
| Early warnings | Gives players time to react | Before turns and obstacles |
| Small adjustments | Keeps movement controlled | Narrow or difficult river sections |
| Balanced teams | Makes races more competitive | Raft Race formats |
| Post-run review | Converts mistakes into practice goals | After failed attempts |

<Callout type="tip" title="Team Communication Tip">
