<Section icon={Route} title="Raft Controls and River Navigation">

The first successful run depends more on controlled movement than aggressive inputs. Get onto the raft, observe how the available controls affect its direction, and make deliberate adjustments. Repeatedly changing direction can make it harder for the group to react when the route narrows or an obstacle approaches.

Look farther ahead than the next turn. A call made too late gives the raft less time to respond, while a short warning before a turn allows the team to act together. In multiplayer, the most useful commands are brief and consistent, such as “left,” “right,” “slow,” or “turn.” Use the same wording throughout the run.

| Navigation Situation | Recommended Response | Common Mistake |
|---|---|---|
| Open river section | Maintain a steady direction | Making unnecessary sharp changes |
| Upcoming turn | Call the direction early | Waiting until the raft reaches the corner |
| Narrow route | Use small adjustments | Overcorrecting repeatedly |
| Unclear obstacle path | Slow the decision and observe | Letting several players choose different routes |
| Team disagreement | Follow the designated caller | Acting independently at the same time |

  </Card>
  <Card title="Call One Direction">

  </Card>
