<Section icon={Map} title="Choosing the Right Mode for Your Group">

The best lobby choice depends on whether your group wants structured progression, direct competition, endurance practice, or a recurring challenge. The available modes support different play styles, so selecting the right one can make the first multiplayer session easier to manage.

| Mode | Main Objective | Best For | Multiplayer Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campaign | Progress through structured river challenges | New players and steady progression | Cooperate and learn the core flow |
| Raft Race | Compete against another team | Competitive groups | Organize 1v1 to 4v4 teams |
| Endless River | Navigate for as long as possible | Practice and endurance | Share movement calls and react ahead |
| Daily River | Attempt the recurring river challenge | Returning players | Coordinate a short group attempt |

For a first session, Campaign is a straightforward choice because it gives the group time to understand the raft and river flow. Endless River is useful when players want to practice reactions without immediately focusing on another team.

Raft Race needs more preparation. Divide players into even teams when possible, agree on one caller per raft, and decide how the group will respond to sharp turns or hazards.

<Section icon={Shield} title="Invite Troubleshooting and Server Checks">

A friend may fail to appear in the lobby for several reasons. The issue may involve account privacy, a different server, a delayed load, or an invitation that was sent before the host entered the game. Use a simple diagnostic order instead of repeatedly sending invites.

| Problem | Likely Cause | Recommended Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Friend cannot join | Privacy or join restrictions | Review Roblox account and privacy settings |
| Friend enters alone | Different server session | Leave and rejoin through the same friend or party route |
| Player is missing | Game still loading | Wait for the experience to finish loading |
| Team is uneven | Players joined at different times | Pause mode selection and reorganize teams |
| Controls feel chaotic | No shared caller | Assign one player to announce directions |
| Race starts too soon | Lobby was not checked | Confirm all players are present before launching |

Use this troubleshooting order:

1. Confirm the host is inside the correct Fart With Your Friends experience.
2. Ask the missing player to verify the game title before joining.
3. Check whether Roblox allows the player to join the host.
4. Have the missing player leave and rejoin the host’s session.
5. Wait until every player is visible before choosing a mode.
6. Restart the session only after the simpler checks fail.

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Multiplayer Coordination Tips and FAQ">

Good lobby organization continues after everyone joins. The raft responds more consistently when players make deliberate adjustments instead of issuing conflicting commands. Keep calls short, look ahead on the river, and decide who has final authority when the group reaches a difficult section.

Use these practical habits:

- Assign one primary caller for each raft.
- Use a backup caller only if the primary player disconnects.
- Announce turns before the raft reaches them.
- Avoid changing direction repeatedly without a shared reason.
- Start with a cooperative activity when the group includes first-time players.
- Use Raft Race team sizes that are easy for everyone to manage.
- Review the previous run before immediately launching another attempt.

| Team Size | Raft Race Format | Coordination Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 2 players | 1v1 | Fast calls and simple direction changes |
| 4 players | 2v2 | One caller per team |
| 6 players | 3v3 | Keep both teams organized before launch |
| 8 players | 4v4 | Confirm team assignments and communication roles |
