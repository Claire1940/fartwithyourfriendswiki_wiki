Once everyone is in the same session, select an activity that matches the group’s experience level. Campaign is a practical starting point for learning the basic flow, while Raft Race is better for direct team competition. Endless River emphasizes continued navigation, and Daily River provides a recurring challenge.

| Activity | Main Objective | Best For | Multiplayer Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campaign | Progress through structured river challenges | New players and steady learning | Practice movement and obstacle awareness |
| Raft Race | Compete against another team | Competitive groups | Assign teams and use coordinated commands |
| Endless River | Continue navigating for as long as possible | Endurance and practice | Focus on survival and consistent steering |
| Daily River | Attempt the recurring daily challenge | Players seeking a repeatable goal | Compare runs and refine the team plan |

A good friends lobby should establish a simple command system before the run. One player can call the next turn, or the team can agree that the player with the clearest view will lead movement decisions.

Useful callouts include:

- **Left** or **right** for an upcoming turn.
- **Slow** when the team needs controlled movement.
- **Obstacle** when a hazard is approaching.
- **Reset** when the current plan is no longer working.
- **Ready** before beginning a competitive round.

<Callout type="success" title="Team Strategy">
For a first group session, start with a cooperative activity or Campaign. Move to Raft Race after everyone understands the controls and can follow one shared direction.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Friends Lobby Troubleshooting and Coordination">

Multiplayer problems usually come from mismatched servers, delayed loading, unclear team assignments, or competing movement decisions. Solve the setup issue first instead of launching multiple rounds and hoping the group eventually synchronizes.

| Problem | Likely Cause | Practical Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Friend is not visible | Different Roblox server | Rejoin through the session leader |
| Friend cannot join | Privacy or party restriction | Review Roblox join and privacy settings |
| Teams are uneven | Players entered at different times | Wait for the full group before choosing teams |
| Raft turns unpredictably | Multiple players give conflicting commands | Assign one primary caller |
| Player loads late | Connection or loading delay | Pause mode selection until the player appears |
| Race feels disorganized | No plan before launch | Agree on commands and team roles first |

During the run, keep communication short. Long explanations are difficult to follow when the river changes quickly. A team should prioritize the next obstacle rather than discussing every previous mistake.
