<Section icon={Shield} title="Teamwork Tips for Better Runs">

Funny challenges become more enjoyable when the team can recover from confusion. The raft does not need constant commands; it needs clear decisions made early enough for everyone to respond.

Use these communication habits:

- **Choose one primary caller.** Multiple players giving opposite directions can create more confusion than the river itself.
- **Use short commands.** “Left,” “right,” “slow,” and “hold” are easier to follow than long explanations during a difficult section.
- **Look ahead.** Call a turn before the obstacle reaches the raft instead of reacting at the last possible moment.
- **Make small adjustments.** Repeatedly changing direction can make the team lose track of the intended route.
- **Review the failure point.** After a mistake, identify whether the problem was timing, unclear communication, or different players following different plans.

| Team Problem | Likely Cause | Practical Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Players give conflicting directions | No agreed caller | Select one primary voice before the run |
| The raft turns too late | Calls happen beside the obstacle | Announce changes while looking ahead |
| Players overcorrect | Movement changes happen too often | Use smaller, deliberate adjustments |
| The team stops communicating | Calls become too long | Use a short shared command list |
| A race feels unfair | Uneven team sizes or unclear rules | Match teams and state the win condition |

For a competitive session, separate **performance points** from **comedy points**. A team might finish first but lose the comedy category because it broke the challenge rule. This gives every group something to enjoy and prevents one failed turn from ending the entire event.

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Challenge Scoring and Replay Ideas">

A simple scoring system makes repeated runs easier to compare. Avoid assigning made-up currency, item values, or official rewards. Instead, use session points that only determine bragging rights within your group.

| Category | Suggested Points | How to Award |
|---|---:|---|
| Completion | 3 | The team completes the selected activity |
| Rule Discipline | 2 | Players follow the challenge rule consistently |
| Communication | 2 | Calls are short, clear, and coordinated |
| Recovery | 1 | The team recovers after a mistake |
| Comedy Moment | 1 | The group agrees that a mistake was especially funny |

After the first run, select one replay variation:

- **Increase the team size:** Add players and introduce a caller or observer role.
- **Change the caller:** Let another player lead the next attempt.
- **Switch the mode:** Move from Campaign practice to Raft Race competition.
- **Reverse the scoring:** Award more points for rule discipline than speed.
- **Create a best-of-three:** Use the same rule across three runs and compare results.

A best-of-three format works especially well for Raft Race because it rewards consistency instead of one lucky attempt. If the teams are uneven, prioritize cooperation and personal improvement rather than treating the result as a formal ranking.

<Callout type="tip" title="Replay Tip">
Change only one variable between attempts. If the mode, team size, rule, and caller all change at once, it becomes difficult to tell which adjustment improved the run.
</Callout>
